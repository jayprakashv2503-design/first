For Kanishka 💖 From Jay Prakash

Our Story

After completing my 10th class, I joined CM Rise School Shujalpur. Her roll number was just after mine, and about one month later, I came to know her.

Slowly, I started falling in love with her. I saw her WhatsApp photo — she was in a pink suit — and sometimes I stared at it for hours.

When my father was diagnosed, I couldn’t go to school until exams, but her photo and name helped reduce my stress every single day.

Our Connection

After completing 11th, during vacation, she accepted my Instagram request — that was the first step in our connection.

We started sharing little moments, our lives, and even Instagram reels. Just one of her reels could make my entire day beautiful, no matter what problems I faced.

Beautiful Memories

When she needed a mobile, I brought it for her. That day, we shook hands for the first time — and honestly, I didn’t wash my hand for the rest of the day, treating it like a lucky charm!

Then came the daal-baati party, where during a truth-or-dare game, I confessed to my friends that I loved her. Everyone teased me to tell her, but I couldn’t gather the courage.

The Day of Confession

On 25th March 2025, the day of our last exam — and her birthday — I came to school fully prepared... not for the paper, but to confess my love. But she had gone to Sehore to celebrate her birthday.

Later, on Ashtami, I finally confessed my feelings to her. That day, she came to know about my love for the first time.

My Eternal Feeling

Even though she hasn’t accepted my feelings yet, my heart still beats the same. I’ll always love her — quietly, truly, and forever.

[translate:तुम मेरी ज़िंदगी की सबसे प्यारी कहानी हो]
("You are the most beautiful story of my life")

Will You Say Yes?

Now, Kanishka, this is for you. You already know my heart, my journey, and my love.
